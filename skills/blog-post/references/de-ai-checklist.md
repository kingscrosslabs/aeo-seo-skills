# De-AI checklist

The fallback when `humanizer` or `no-ai-slop` isn't installed. Run it twice: once as a rewrite pass (fix as you go), then again as a detect pass on the result. Don't touch the format rules: bold lead-ins, bold key terms, bullets and tables stay. Don't change a fact or a question-phrased heading; rephrase around it.

## Words and phrases

- [ ] **Hype and filler words.** Cut or replace: seamless, robust, leverage, unlock, empower, elevate, streamline, cutting-edge, game-changing, revolutionize, delve, landscape, realm, tapestry, testament, pivotal, crucial, vital, navigate (figuratively), journey, embark.
- [ ] **Throat-clearing.** Cut: "It's worth noting that", "It's important to remember", "In today's fast-paced world", "When it comes to", "At the end of the day", "In conclusion".
- [ ] **Vague attribution.** "Experts say", "many believe", "studies show" with no named source. Name the source or cut the claim.
- [ ] **Inflated significance.** "plays a crucial role in", "stands as a testament to", "marks a pivotal moment". Say what it does.

## Sentence patterns

- [ ] **"Not just X, but Y"** and "It's not X, it's Y". Say Y.
- [ ] **Rule of three.** Lists of three adjectives or three parallel clauses that exist for rhythm. Keep the one that carries meaning.
- [ ] **Trailing -ing clauses** that add a vague comment: "…, ensuring a smooth experience", "…, highlighting the importance of…". Cut them or make them their own sentence with a fact.
- [ ] **Em dashes.** At most one per paragraph. Use a period, colon or comma instead.
- [ ] **Stacked transitions.** "Moreover", "Furthermore", "Additionally" opening sentence after sentence. Cut most of them.
- [ ] **Rhetorical questions** answered in the next sentence, outside of H2s. Make the statement.
- [ ] **Uniform rhythm.** Every sentence the same length. Mix short and long.

## Content

- [ ] **Generic examples.** Swap "for example, a company might…" for a specific case from research or the experience bank.
- [ ] **Hedging.** "may potentially", "can often help to". Say what's true, or cut it.
- [ ] **Empty summary endings.** A final paragraph that restates the post. End on the next step or the CTA instead.
- [ ] **Praise for the reader or the question.** "Great question", "You're not alone". Cut it.
