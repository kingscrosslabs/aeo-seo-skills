# Interview rounds

Ask two or three questions at a time and wait for the answers. Skip any question the repo, the live site, OpenSEO's project context or a linked doc already answered, and say which ones you skipped.

## Round 1: the goal
1. What should search do for this site? (Signups, demo requests, sales, newsletter subscribers.)
2. Is there a search engine or AI engine you care about most?

## Round 2: the product
1. In one sentence, what does the product do, for whom?
2. What's the problem that makes someone go searching for it? What do they type?

## Round 3: the reader
1. Who is the buyer the pages should speak to: role, company, situation?
2. Who finds you through search but isn't a fit? (These become queries to skip.)

## Round 4: what's different
1. What 2–4 things do you do better than the alternatives, that you could prove on a page?
2. Who do people compare you to? (Names only. Facts get sourced later.)

## Round 5: facts and voice
1. Where do the true numbers live: pricing, what's live, approved claims, docs?
2. How should the site sound? Any words you never want on it?

## Round 6: proof and experience (optional)
1. Any first-hand stories, product stats or customer quotes you'd be happy to publish? Each one goes into `experience-bank.md` as `pending` until you approve it.
2. Who can be the byline on pages, and who writes what?

## Round 7: shipping
1. How does a change go live? Who has to review it?
2. Anything an agent must never do in this repo? (Push to main, run a build with side effects, edit certain files.)
