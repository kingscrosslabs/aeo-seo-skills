## Strategy

### What it is

A set of open-source agent skills that do AEO (answer engine optimization) and
SEO as one job. They run in Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and any agent that reads
`SKILL.md` files. They plan topics, audit the site, and write blog posts,
comparison pages, feature pages and use-case pages that rank in Google and get
quoted by ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews.

They are the skills King's Cross Labs uses every week on its own products.

### The problem

Most SEO advice was written for ten blue links. AI answer engines now read a
page, lift one paragraph, and cite it or don't. Teams either keep writing for
the old game, or hand the job to an AI writer that invents numbers and sounds
like everyone else. Both lose.

### Audience

**Primary:** founders and marketers at small companies who own their website
and already use an AI coding agent. They can run a terminal command. They don't
have an SEO team and don't want an agency.

**What they believe:** AI search matters now, and their site isn't showing up
in it.

**What they're skeptical of:** SEO tools that guess numbers, AI content that
reads like AI content, and anything that publishes without asking.

**Not for:** agencies looking for a white-label content mill, or anyone who
wants pages shipped without reading them.

### Positioning

**Category:** open-source agent skills for AEO and SEO.

**Differentiators**

- **AEO and SEO are one job.** Every page is written answer-first so it ranks
  and survives being quoted out of context.
- **Real data, never guessed.** Search numbers come from OpenSEO and Google
  Search Console. If the data isn't connected, the skill stops.
- **You approve the work.** Briefs and pages wait for a yes before they ship.
- **Your context stays yours.** Everything about your site lives in an
  `aeo-seo/` folder in your project. Updating the skills never touches it.
- **Free and open source.** MIT licence.

### Personality

Direct. Specific. Proof over promise. Builder to builder.

It is not: an SEO guru, a growth hack, or a tool that promises rankings.

## Voice

Follows the KCL company voice: direct, sharp, warm when it's earned, never
corporate, never over-hedged.

### Rules for this brand

- Say what the skill does in one plain sentence before saying why it matters.
- Name the real tools and engines: Google, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI
  Overviews, OpenSEO, Search Console. Vague "AI platforms" is banned.
- Use numbers only when they are true and current (skill count, check count).
- Short sentences. Colons over dashes. No exclamation marks.
- Write "AEO and SEO" in prose; "AEO-SEO" only as a name or label.
- Always spell out AEO on first use on a page: answer engine optimization.

### Vocabulary

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| get found, get quoted, rank | dominate, crush, 10x |
| skills | prompts, hacks, templates |
| your agent | the AI, the bot |
| you approve | fully automated, hands-free |
| real search data | AI-powered insights |
| open source, free | freemium, get started for free |

Banned outright: unlock, supercharge, game-changer, revolutionize, seamless,
leverage (as a verb), in today's fast-paced world.

### Taglines

- Primary: **Get found in Google. Get quoted by AI.**
- Hero: **AEO and SEO skills for AI agents.**
- Short: **Open-source AEO and SEO for your agent.**

## Governance

### Naming

- The product is **AEO-SEO Skills** in titles and **aeo-seo-skills** as the
  repo and wordmark.
- Skill names are always written in code style and exactly as the folder:
  `blog-post`, not "Blog Post skill".

### Claims

- Never promise rankings, traffic or citations. Say what the skill does.
- A skill's status on the site must match the README: Available, In progress
  or Coming soon.
- Agent compatibility claims must be true: only list agents that load
  `SKILL.md` skills.
- Credit: "Built by Mika Reyes at King's Cross Labs."

### Calls to action

Every primary CTA is **Star on GitHub** and links to the repo. No email capture,
no waitlist, no pricing.
